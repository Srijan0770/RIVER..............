About RiverScan

Understanding River Water Quality
Through Data

RiverScan turns raw pH readings into actionable insights — helping researchers, analysts, and environmentalists make sense of river health across cities.

The Project

What is RiverScan?

RiverScan is a web-based water quality analysis tool that processes river pH data from a structured CSV dataset. Search for any river by name and instantly get a full breakdown — summary statistics, city-wise comparisons, safety classifications, and auto-generated visualizations.

How It Works

From search to insight in seconds

🔍

Fuzzy Search

Type any river name — even with typos. RapidFuzz matches your input against the dataset using similarity scoring.

📊

Data Processing

Pandas aggregates pH readings by city, computing averages, min/max values, and reading counts per location.

📈

Chart Generation

Matplotlib renders line graphs, bar charts, pie charts, and category breakdowns — saved as PNGs and served instantly.

✅

Safety Report

Every reading is classified and the best/worst cities are surfaced so you can act on the data right away.

Classification

pH Safety Scale

RiverScan classifies every pH reading into one of three categories based on WHO drinking water guidelines.

pH < 6.5
Acidic (Unsafe)
6.5 – 8.5
Normal (Safe)
pH > 8.5
Alkaline (Unsafe)
